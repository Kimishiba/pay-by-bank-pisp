Strategic Finding: Europe's Geopolitical Standards Split KEY TAKEAWAY

Under PSD2, European retail banking divided into two non-interoperable regional technical standards: Berlin Group NextGenPSD2 in Germany (~100%), the Netherlands (~90%+), and domestic Belgium (~70%); and STET PSD2 across France (~95%).

The Breakthrough: Building a single STET adapter in the CM.com Gateway unlocks virtually the entire French retail market (BNP Paribas, Crédit Agricole, Société Générale, BPCE, and Crédit Mutuel) without requiring custom code for individual banks. With our existing Berlin Group engine and a companion STET engine, CM Payments B.V. achieves sovereign coverage of over 85% of Western European transaction accounts.

Strategic Recommendation for CM Payments B.V. ACTION PLAN
  1. Maintain Berlin Group Engine: Deploy immediate coverage for the Netherlands and Germany (~1,100 institutions across Sparkassen and Volksbanken with zero per-bank development).
  2. Develop Modular STET Adapter: Build adapters/stet inside the Go gateway. One adapter fulfills the requirements of all Top 6 French banking networks.
  3. Consolidated eIDAS Credentials: Retain a single QWAC/QSealC certificate pair from an accredited QTSP (e.g. QuoVadis or DigiCert) to satisfy both Berlin Group and STET security mandates.

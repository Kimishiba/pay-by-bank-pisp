01

Baseline Regulatory Standing & Scope

CM Payments B.V. is already fully authorized by De Nederlandsche Bank (DNB) as a Payment Institution (Betaalinstelling) for Service 3 (payment transaction execution) and Service 5 (issuing and acquiring). As an active licensed institution, CM.com already absorbs the baseline corporate, prudential, and supervisory overhead of holding a financial license.

Adding Payment Initiation Services (PISP - Service 7) does not establish a new financial entity. Instead, it is executed via a formal Application for Scope Extension (Aanvraag tot uitbreiding van de vergunning) under Section 2:3b of the Dutch Financial Supervision Act (Wft). Consequently, all corporate baseline expenses remain fixed, and only the net incremental delta applies.

The Incremental Advantage
Because CM Payments B.V. already holds a full PI license, Initial Capital requirements, DNB base supervisory levies, executive director vetting, and core annual compliance audits incur €0 in extra costs. The net recurring delta to maintain Service 7 is restricted to mandatory PII insurance, eIDAS certificates, and cloud KMS infrastructure (~€19.5k–€48.5k/year).

Action Plan: The 4 Steps to Secure Service 7

1
Procure Mandatory PISP PII Policy Rider
Engage insurance brokers (Marsh / Aon / Lloyd's) to add an EBA/GL/2017/08 compliant PISP liability endorsement to CM Payments B.V.'s policy and obtain the official DNB Insurance Declaration (Verzekeringsverklaring).
2
Draft PIS Program of Operations & DORA Risk Annex
Update the existing risk framework with specific PSD2 Article 66 non-handling declarations and Berlin Group technical architecture specifications.
3
Submit Scope Extension Application via MijnDNB
File the statutory Form *Uitbreiding vergunning betaalinstelling* on DNB’s Digital Supervision Portal and settle the non-refundable €2,700 application tariff.
4
Procure eIDAS QWAC & QSealC Certificates from QTSP
Upon DNB approval decree, obtain ETSI TS 119 495 certificates (QuoVadis / DigiCert) containing the new `PSP_PI` role for production mTLS connectivity with Dutch and EU banks.
